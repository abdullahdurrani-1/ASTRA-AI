# The Harbour Lighthouse of Port Vellan

Port Vellan is a fictional fishing town used to test Astra AI. Its lighthouse was completed in 1874 by the engineer Miriam Okafor. The tower is 38 metres tall and was built from local grey granite.

The original lamp burned whale oil until 1902, when it was replaced by a paraffin vapour burner. Electricity arrived in 1951, and the light became fully automatic in 1989.

# The Keepers

Seven head keepers served at the lighthouse. The longest-serving keeper was Tomas Reyes, who worked there for 27 years, from 1921 to 1948.

Keepers climbed 212 steps to reach the lantern room. They recorded weather observations every four hours in a logbook, which is now held in the Port Vellan Maritime Archive.

# The Optics

The lighthouse uses a second-order Fresnel lens made in Paris. The lens flashes white twice every 15 seconds, a pattern sailors write as Fl(2) W 15s. In clear weather the light is visible for 22 nautical miles.

In 1967 a storm cracked one of the lens prisms. It was repaired using a replacement prism cast in 1968.

# Conservation

The lighthouse became a protected heritage site in 2004. A visitor centre opened in 2011 and receives about 40,000 visitors each year. Entry fees fund the annual repainting of the tower, which is done every spring.
